Education plan

For schools, colleges and universities.

Teach parametric design with real, printable parts: a few lines of Python, a bin that fits the classroom drawers. The Education plan gives your institution Builder limits and a key for every class. Public schools get it at a price that only covers our costs; school budgets are tight enough.

  • Apply like everyone else. Tell us about the institution and what you'll use it for.
  • Show us it's real. A letter on official letterhead signed by your principal, head or dean, or a notarised or certified statement (or your country's usual way of proving it), plus a staff ID or the institution's registration.
  • We check every one by hand. You'll see the decision in your console. Your documents are only used for the check and are deleted 30 days after we decide.
